About me.

I'm a UX/UI designer and front-end developer. I take ideas from the first Figma sketch to a live website or app.

My passion for design and creativity started in childhood when I attended art classes for about 10 years. Although I took a break from drawing and art, I constantly noticed creative ideas and inspirations in everyday life.

Discovering UX/UI design and coding helped me reconnect with my artistic side, allowing me to bring ideas to life in a functional and digital way.
